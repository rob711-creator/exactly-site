Common questions

My group won't clear. The group has to be the exact size of its number — not more, not fewer. Four touching 3s will not clear; exactly three will. Tapping a group that cannot clear costs you nothing and tells you why.

I lost my scores. Scores are saved on your device only, so uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them permanently. There is no account to restore from — which is also why nothing about you is ever sent anywhere.

The daily challenge won't let me replay. That is intentional: everyone gets the same board each day and one attempt, so the scores mean something. A new board arrives at midnight, your local time.

No sound. Check the speaker button on the home screen, then your device's silent switch and volume. Sound, music and vibration each have their own toggle in Stats & Settings.

How do I replay the tutorial? "? How to play" on the home screen, any time.
